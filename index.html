Our story

About The Harsha Crafts & Gifts

The Harsha Crafts & Gifts is a custom gifting and craft studio, built around one idea: a gift should feel like it was made for exactly one person. What began as a small collection of handmade, personalized pieces has grown into a following of 302K+ on Instagram (@the_harsha_crafts), with 5,176 posts of creations shared with a community across India.

Every order is treated as one-of-a-kind — a name etched into wood, a photo turned into a keepsake, a message tucked into a handmade card. The studio works closely with each customer over WhatsApp to get every detail right before anything is crafted.

This website is a demo concept created to show how that same personal experience could work as a proper online store — so an Instagram visitor can go from discovery to a confirmed, customized order without leaving a single conversation thread.
