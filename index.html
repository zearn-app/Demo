TAMIL MOVIES

AADHI

The movie was directed by Ramana, with Trisha playing the female lead. The story follows Aathi, a young man who comes to Chennai to pursue his studies and live with his grandparents. As the story develops, Aathi discovers that his family has a painful and dangerous past. He learns about the enemies who were responsible for the death of his parents and decides to protect his family and fight against them. The movie combines action, romance, family emotions, revenge, and suspense. Vijay's character is shown as brave, caring, and determined to protect the people he loves. Trisha's Anjali, who becomes an important person in Aathi's life, also plays a major role. The film also has several action sequences, songs, and dance performances by Vijay. Vidyasagar composed the music for the movie. Overall, Aathi is a film that focuses on family relationships, courage, love, and the fight against enemies.

SHAJAHAN

A Tamil romantic drama film released in 2001, starring Vijay in the lead role and directed by Ravi. The movie focuses on the theme of love and the different emotions connected with relationships. Vijay plays the character of Raja, a man who helps people find their true love and acts as a guide for couples facing difficulties in their relationships. However, while helping others with their love lives, Raja himself experiences the pain and confusion of love. The story becomes emotional when he falls in love and realizes that love is not always simple or easy. The movie explores friendship, sacrifice, unrequited love, and the importance of understanding another person's feelings. The film also features Richa Pallod as the female lead, along with other supporting actors. The music was composed by Mani Sharma, and the songs became an important part of the movie's romantic atmosphere.

SACHIN

Sachin is a 2005 Tamil romantic comedy film directed by John Mahendran and starring Vijay in the lead role, alongside Genelia D'Souza and Bipasha Basu. The story follows Sachin, a cheerful and kind-hearted college student who develops feelings for a girl named Shalini. The film explores love, friendship, and youthful relationships in a light-hearted way. Vijay's charming performance, Genelia's lively portrayal, and the film's entertaining music contributed to its popularity among Tamil cinema audiences.

MALAYALAM

PREMALU

A 2024 Malayalam-language romantic comedy directed by Girish A. D. and produced by Dileesh Pothan, Fahadh Faasil, and Syam Pushkaran. The film stars Naslen K. Gafoor, Mamitha Baiju, Sangeeth Prathap, Shyam Mohan, and Akhila Bhargavan in important roles. It follows Sachin, a young man who travels to Hyderabad with hopes of starting a new chapter in his life. There, he meets Reenu, an ambitious and confident young woman, and gradually falls in love with her. However, Reenu sees Sachin mainly as a friend, creating a humorous and emotionally relatable situation. Much of the film's charm comes from its natural conversations, youthful characters, friendship, and everyday situations rather than a complicated plot. The Hyderabad setting also plays an important role, giving the movie a fresh urban atmosphere.

BANGALORE DAYS

Bangalore Days is a popular Malayalam-language coming-of-age drama film directed by Anjali Menon and released in 2014. The film was produced by Anwar Rasheed and features a talented ensemble cast including Dulquer Salmaan, Nivin Pauly, Nazriya Nazim, Fahadh Faasil, Parvathy Thiruvothu, Isha Talwar, and Kalpana. The story revolves around three cousins—Krishnan P. P. (Kuttan), Arjun (Aju), and Divya—who share a close bond from childhood and later move to Bangalore, where they experience new relationships, challenges, and personal transformations. Divya, played by Nazriya Nazim, is an energetic young woman who dreams of experiencing an independent and exciting life in Bangalore. After her marriage to Das, played by Fahadh Faasil, she moves to the city and begins adjusting to her new life. Das is a successful businessman who has a complicated personality and prefers to maintain emotional distance.

KUMBALANGI NIGHTS

A 2019 Malayalam-language drama film directed by Madhu C. Narayanan and written by Syam Pushkaran. The film stars Fahadh Faasil, Soubin Shahir, Shane Nigam, Sreenath Bhasi, Anna Ben, and Mathew Thomas. The story is set in the beautiful coastal village of Kumbalangi near Kochi and focuses on four brothers—Saji, Bonny, Bobby, and Franky—who live together in a troubled and dysfunctional family. Each brother has a different personality and struggles with loneliness, anger, relationships, and the difficulties of everyday life. The film mainly explores the complicated relationship between the brothers and how they slowly learn to care for and support one another.

ENGLISH MOVIES

THE BEAUTIFUL MIND

A Beautiful Mind is a 2001 American biographical drama film directed by Ron Howard and starring Russell Crowe, Jennifer Connelly, Ed Harris, and Paul Bettany. The film is inspired by the life of John Nash, a brilliant mathematician who makes an important contribution to game theory while struggling with severe mental illness. The story begins with Nash as a young and ambitious student at Princeton University, where he searches for an original mathematical idea that will establish his reputation. His dedication eventually leads to a groundbreaking theory, bringing him academic recognition and professional success. However, Nash's life becomes increasingly complicated as he begins experiencing disturbing visions and believes that he is involved in a secret government mission. The film explores intelligence, love, determination, mental health, reality, and perseverance.

THE NOTEBOOK

A 2004 American romantic drama film directed by Nick Cassavetes and based on the novel by Nicholas Sparks. The film stars Ryan Gosling as Noah Calhoun and Rachel McAdams as Allie Hamilton. The story follows Noah and Allie, two young people from very different social backgrounds who fall deeply in love during the summer of 1940. Although their relationship is passionate, Allie's wealthy family does not approve of Noah, and the couple eventually separates. Years later, Allie becomes engaged to another man, while Noah continues to hold on to their memories and the love they shared. Their paths cross again, forcing Allie to confront her feelings and decide between the life she has built and the love she has never completely forgotten.

AVATAR

A 2009 science-fiction adventure film directed and written by James Cameron. The movie stars Sam Worthington as Jake Sully, Zoe Saldana as Neytiri, Sigourney Weaver as Dr. Grace Augustine, Stephen Lang as Colonel Miles Quaritch, and Michelle Rodriguez as Trudy Chacon. The story is set in the year 2154 on the beautiful alien moon Pandora, which is inhabited by a blue-skinned humanoid species called the Na'vi. Humans travel to Pandora to obtain a valuable mineral called unobtanium, which is important for Earth's energy needs. Jake Sully is a former Marine who becomes involved in the Avatar Program after his twin brother dies. The program allows humans to control genetically engineered Na'vi-human hybrids called Avatars.
